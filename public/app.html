    Create your company's workspace

    A workspace is your company's private space in Greenlight. It holds your people, their requests, approval chains, budgets and the full record of every decision. Nobody outside it can see anything inside it. You'll be its Admin, and you invite everyone else.

    • Name it after your company, the way your colleagues know it. For example: Acme Inc.
    • Only one team using Greenlight? Add the team. For example: Acme – Marketing
    • Everyone you invite sees this name on their invitation and at the top of the app.

      Departments and budgets

      Requests count toward the requester's department. Greenlight flags requests that would take a department past 80% or 100% of its budget.

      Currency

      Reports and budgets are in the base currency. Requests in other currencies are converted with these rates.

      Slack

      Post new approvals, decisions and budget alerts to a Slack channel. In Slack, create an incoming webhook for the channel and paste its URL here.

      Time off

      Vacation days per calendar year for everyone. You can give individual people a different number on the Time off tab. Only business days (Monday to Friday) count.

      Reminders and escalation

      When an approver doesn't answer, Greenlight reminds them, and later moves the step to their manager (or an Admin).

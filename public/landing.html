Features

Everything a request needs, from "can I?" to "approved"

One place for requests, the right approvers every time, and a clear record of who decided what.

Approval chains

Decide who approves what. Send big purchases to Finance automatically, and small ones straight to the manager.

Smart risk checks

Unusual amounts, new vendors, duplicates and split purchases are flagged before anyone clicks approve.

Roles that make sense

Employees, managers and admins each see exactly what they need. Nobody can approve their own request.

Full audit trail

Every request, approval and change is recorded and can't be edited. Ready when your accountant asks.

Invite in seconds

Add your team with a secure invite link. Links expire after 7 days and only work for the person invited.

Secure by default

Sign in with Google, no new passwords. Each company's data stays in its own private workspace.

Budgets and dashboards

Set budgets per department and get warned at 80%. See spending by month, vendor and person, in any currency, and export it to Excel.

Time off, sorted

Vacation balances that count business days, a team calendar of who's out, and a delegate who approves while you're away.

Nothing gets stuck

Reminders when an approver goes quiet, automatic escalation, receipts on every request, and updates in the app, by email and in Slack.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Greenlight helps companies submit and approve requests (purchases, expenses and time off). This page explains what information the service collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics, when you sign in with Google: your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID. We do not get your Google password and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • What you and your colleagues enter: workspace names, members and roles, invitations, requests (titles, descriptions, amounts, vendors, dates), approval decisions and comments.
  • An activity record (audit log) of actions in each workspace, so Admins can see who did what and when.
  • A sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in. It is strictly necessary and not used for tracking or advertising.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and show you the workspaces you belong to.
  • To route requests to the right approvers and send related emails (invitations, approval needed, approved or rejected), when email is enabled.
  • To flag requests that may need a closer look (for example an unusually high amount or a new vendor).

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Information inside a workspace is visible only to members of that workspace, according to their role. People outside a workspace cannot see it.

We use these service providers to run the service:

  • Railway hosts the application and its database.
  • Google provides sign-in.
  • An email provider (Brevo or Resend), only if email sending is enabled.
  • Anthropic, only if AI review is enabled: it receives a request's type, title, description, amount, vendor and dates (never names or email addresses) to suggest a category and risk level.

How long we keep it

Information is kept while your workspace uses the service. Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days and invitation links after 7 days. A workspace Admin can remove members. To have your account or a workspace deleted, contact us (below).

Your choices

You can stop using the service at any time and revoke the app's access in your Google account permissions. You can ask us for a copy of your information or to delete it.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: open an issue at github.com/avitalde13/approval-workflows.
